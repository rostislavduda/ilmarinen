\begin{abstract}
This is a reference catalogue of what the \code{ilmarinen} package computes, expressed symbolically. It
enumerates the eight computational \emph{contracts} (contracts), the \code{AllGraph} controller and its
selection operators, every \emph{schema} and its meta-cell algebra, and every \emph{primitive} with
the exact tensor operation it realizes. Notation follows the analytical report (\emph{the action}
$J=R+\mu\Omega$; the mixture weight $\alphapar$ as a point on the primitive simplex) and the
implementation report (module and symbol names in \code{typewriter}). The goal is a compact map from
code element to mathematical operation, not a derivation; derivations live in the other two reports and
are cited by section name where relevant.
\end{abstract}

\section{Common objects and the schema algebra}
\label{sec:algebra}

\paragraph{Data container.} A dataset is wrapped in \code{AllData}, which fixes the \emph{type} by how it
is built: \code{dense\_tensor}$(X)$ for a fixed-size array $X$ (a grid of some rank), \code{graphs}$(\{V_i\},\{E_i\})$
for node features with edges, \code{point\_sets}$(\{V_i\})$ for unordered sets, and \code{functions}$(a,y)$
for field-valued input/target pairs sampled on a grid (the neural-operator interface). Positions
$\{\bm{x}_i\}\subset\R^3$ may accompany graphs/sets to signal geometry.

\paragraph{Primitive.} A primitive is a map $f_p:\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{X}$ on a contract's carrier space,
type-preserving so that primitives compose across depth and mix within a layer. Each contract $c$ has a
finite \emph{vocabulary} $\Pcal_c=\{f_p\}$; the vocabularies are catalogued in \S\ref{sec:seq}--\S\ref{sec:operator}.

\paragraph{Meta-cell (the relaxation).} A cell places the vocabulary in parallel and mixes it by a softmax
over a learnable logit vector $\alphapar\in\R^{|\Pcal_c|}$. Writing $w=\softmax(\alphapar)$, the cell map is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:cell}
\Phi_{\alphapar}(h)\;=\;\sum_{p\in\Pcal_c} w_p\, f_p(h),
\qquad w_p=\frac{e^{\alpha_p}}{\sum_{q}e^{\alpha_q}} .
\end{equation}
Every contract reuses \eqref{eq:cell} verbatim; the cell classes are
\code{UnifiedCell}, \code{\_GraphCell},
\code{\_EquivCell}, \code{UnifiedSetCell}, \code{\_OperatorCell}, and so on, differing only in the
carrier space and $\Pcal_c$. The mixture is a convex combination on the simplex $\Delta^{|\Pcal_c|-1}$,
so at a vertex ($w\to e_p$) the cell collapses to a single primitive.

\paragraph{Schema (depth composition).} A depth-$L$ schema stacks $L$ independent cells; layer
$\ell$ consumes the previous layer's mixed output,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:depth}
h^{(0)}=\text{input},\qquad h^{(\ell)}=\Phi^{(\ell)}_{\alphapar^{(\ell)}}\!\big(h^{(\ell-1)}\big),\quad \ell=1,\dots,L,
\end{equation}
each layer carrying its own $\alphapar^{(\ell)}$ (independent per-layer selection). A read-out head $g$ maps
the top representation to the prediction $\hat y=g(h^{(L)})$ (\S\ref{sec:readout}). The first layer maps
input width $\Cin$ to hidden width $K$; subsequent layers are $K\to K$.

\paragraph{The action.} All selection in \code{AllGraph} minimizes a single description-length action
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:action}
J \;=\; R \;+\; \mu\,\Omega,
\end{equation}
with $R$ the empirical risk (cross-entropy or MSE) and $\Omega$ a complexity charge; the different
selection stages (\S\ref{sec:select}) are instances of \eqref{eq:action} with different $\Omega$
(structure, width, depth, mixture sparsity, spectral modes). The price $\mu$ is the accuracy/complexity
exchange rate (\emph{Pareto slope}); $\mu=0$ recovers pure fit.

\section{Contracts and the \texorpdfstring{$\code{AllGraph}$}{AllGraph} controller}
\label{sec:contracts}

\paragraph{The eight contracts.} \code{AllGraph.\_ARENAS} enumerates the contracts, each an equivariance/interface
class with its own carrier space, symmetry group $G$, and vocabulary:
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
contract & carrier & symmetry $G$ & \S \\
\midrule
\code{sequence}    & $(b,T,\Cin)$ time series           & time translation (soft)     & \ref{sec:seq}\\
\code{spatial}     & $(b,\Cin,H,W)$ image               & 2D translation              & \ref{sec:grid}\\
\code{volumetric}  & $(b,\Cin,D,H,W)$ volume            & 3D translation              & \ref{sec:grid}\\
\code{4d}          & $(b,\Cin,T,D,H,W)$ space--time     & 4D translation              & \ref{sec:grid}\\
\code{graph}       & nodes $(N,F)$ + edges $E$          & node permutation $\Sn$      & \ref{sec:graph}\\
\code{equivariant} & geometric graph $(s,\bm v,\bm x,E)$& $\Sn\times\SO$              & \ref{sec:equiv}\\
\code{set}         & elements $(N,F)$ + membership      & element permutation $\Sn$   & \ref{sec:set}\\
\code{operator}    & fields $a(\bm x)\!\mapsto\!u(\bm x)$& discretization-invariance  & \ref{sec:operator}\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Routing $\rho$.} \code{route}$(\Dcal)$ maps data to a contract by its kinematics: rank and
structure of the tensor decide the grid family (\code{sequence}/\code{spatial}/\code{volumetric}/\code{4d}),
presence of edges lifts to \code{graph}, positions lift to \code{equivariant}, edgeless node collections
route to \code{set}, and field pairs to \code{operator}. A flat vector $(b,d)$ routes to \code{sequence}
(unstructured rank-1). If the routed contract is disabled, \code{\_resolve\_enabled\_fallback} degrades along a
constructibility order (e.g. $\code{equivariant}\!\to\!\code{graph}\!\to\!\code{set}\!\to\!\code{sequence}$),
dropping the structure the fallback cannot honor.

\paragraph{Tie-break $\tau$.} When several contracts are admissible (e.g.\ a geometric graph could be run as
\code{set}, \code{graph}, or \code{equivariant}), \code{tiebreak} trains each admissible contract solo and
selects by the priced action
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:tiebreak}
c^\star=\arg\min_{c}\;\Big[\,R(c)+\mu_c\,\Omega_{\mathrm{struct}}(c)\,\Big],
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the contract whose structural prior pays for itself in fit (\code{machinery/contract\_mdl.py};
Bayesian-evidence reading in \code{contract\_evidence.py}).

\paragraph{Controller API.} \code{AllGraph} exposes: \code{route} and \code{tiebreak} (contract choice),
\code{select\_architecture} and \code{select\_architecture\_by\_area} (size choice, \S\ref{sec:select}),
\code{fit} (build the chosen schema, train, optionally price the mixture and discretize),
\code{apply\_canonicalization} (pose alignment for geometric data), and \code{explain} (attribution/report).
The controller holds width $K$, depth $L$, seed, and the enabled-contract restriction.

\noindent The mixture \eqref{eq:cell} contracts the $P$ per-primitive sequence outputs,
$\Phi_{\alphapar}(x)=\sum_p w_p f_p(x)$ (an \code{einsum} $p,pbtw\!\to\!btw$). When every selected core is
recurrent, the stack streams one timestep at a time (\code{step}); a nonrecurrent core
(\code{conv}/\code{dilconv}/\code{attention}/\code{dense}/\code{norm}/\code{spectral}) forces whole-sequence
evaluation.

\section{Grid contracts: spatial, volumetric, 4d}
\label{sec:grid}

\section{Graph contract}
\label{sec:graph}

Carrier: node features $X\in\R^{N\times F}$, directed edges $E$ with rows $[\text{src},\text{dst}]$ (messages
flow src$\to$dst); module \code{graph\_schema.py}. Let $\mathcal{N}(v)=\{u:(u,v)\in E\}$ and let
$\bigoplus$ denote a permutation-invariant neighbor aggregation. All cores map $\R^{N\times F}\!\to\!\R^{N\times K}$
and are $\Sn$-equivariant (a node permutation permutes the rows identically).

the single-aggregator injectivity gap.

\section{Equivariant contract}
\label{sec:equiv}

Carrier: geometric graph with type-0 (scalar) features $s\in\R^{N\times C_0}$ and type-1 (vector) features
$\bm v\in\R^{N\times C_1\times 3}$, positions $\bm x\in\R^{N\times 3}$, edges $E$; module
\code{equivariant\_graph\_schema.py}. All cores are $\SO$-equivariant and type-preserving:
under $\bm x\mapsto \mathbf{R}\bm x$, scalars are invariant and vectors rotate, $\bm v\mapsto \bm v\mathbf{R}^\top$.
Edge geometry enters through the unit direction $\rhat_{uv}=(\bm x_v-\bm x_u)/\lVert\bm x_v-\bm x_u\rVert$ and
length $d_{uv}$.

\paragraph{Equivariant building blocks.} Type-preserving linear \code{\_EquivLinear}: $s\mapsto sW_s+b_s$,
$\bm v\mapsto \bm v\,W_v$ (channel mix shared across the $2\ell{+}1$ components, no vector bias). Gated
nonlinearity \code{\_GatedNonlin}: $s\mapsto\tanh s$, $\bm v\mapsto \sig(\text{Lin}(s))\odot\bm v$ (an
\emph{invariant} gate scales vectors, preserving direction). Norm \code{\_EquivNorm}: $\mathrm{LN}(s)$ on
scalars; $\bm v$ divided by the RMS of its per-channel invariant norms $\lVert\bm v_c\rVert$.

\paragraph{Clebsch--Gordan message.} \code{\_tensor\_product\_message} couples source features with the edge
spherical harmonics $Y_0{=}1,\,Y_1{=}\rhat$ for $\ell\le1$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:cg}
\underbrace{s\text{-out}}_{0}\!:\ \{\,s_u,\ \langle \bm v_u,\rhat\rangle\,\},\qquad
\underbrace{\bm v\text{-out}}_{1}\!:\ \{\,s_u\,\rhat,\ \bm v_u\times\rhat\,\},
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the CG paths~\cite{thomas2018tfn,geiger2022e3nn} $0{\times}0{\to}0$, $1{\times}1{\to}0$ (dot), $0{\times}1{\to}1$ (scale), $1{\times}1{\to}1$
(cross). Table \ref{tab:equiv} lists the eight cores.

\emph{approximate} equivariance (a free non-equivariant pathway gated by a breaking-power price) in
\code{models/approximate\_equivariance.py}.

\section{Set contract}
\label{sec:set}

Carrier: elements $X\in\R^{N\times F}$ with a batch map $b:\{1{:}N\}\to\{1{:}n_{\mathrm{sets}}\}$; module
\code{set\_schema.py}. Cores are $\Sn$-\emph{equivariant} element maps $\R^{N\times F}\!\to\!\R^{N\times K}$;
a permutation-\emph{invariant} pool at readout gives the per-set output (Deep Sets~\cite{zaheer2017deepsets} factorization
$\rho(\bigoplus_i\phi(x_i))$).

\begin{tabular}{@{}p{1.9cm}p{11.0cm}@{}}

\midrule

\noindent Multihead attention \code{\_MHA} masks cross-set pairs, $\text{scores}_{qk}=\langle q,k\rangle/\sqrt{d_k}$

$\bigoplus\in\{\text{sum},\text{mean},\text{max},\text{FSPool}\}$ selects the invariant readout, where
FSPool~\cite{zhang2020fspool} sorts each feature across elements and reads an order-weighted descriptor (retains distributional
shape, not just a moment).

\section{Operator contract}
\label{sec:operator}

Carrier: input field $a(\bm x)$ and target field $u(\bm x)$ sampled on a grid, lifted to a channel field
$h\in\R^{b\times K\times\text{grid}}$; module \code{operator\_schema.py}. Cores are
resolution-independent maps $h\mapsto h$ (a trained operator transfers zero-shot across grid resolutions).
Let $\mathcal F$ be the (real) FFT along the spatial axes.

\footnotesize

\midrule

per-primitive mode budget $m$ is itself a priced degree of freedom (\S\ref{sec:select}, spectral selection).

\section{Selection operators (instances of \texorpdfstring{$J=R+\mu\Omega$}{J=R+muOmega})}
\label{sec:select}

The controller turns the fixed hyperparameters (contract, width, depth, mixture support, mode count) into
\emph{outputs} of priced measurements. Each is a marginal-value rule on \eqref{eq:action}.

\paragraph{Contract.} \eqref{eq:tiebreak}: argmin over admissible contracts of $R(c)+\mu_c\Omega_{\mathrm{struct}}(c)$;
$\Omega_{\mathrm{struct}}$ is the description length of the contract's structural prior
(\code{contract\_mdl.py}), with a Bayesian-evidence equivalent (\code{contract\_evidence.py}). Optionally
(\code{price\_singular}, \code{singular\_mdl.py}) the charge is augmented by the \emph{functional} code
length $\omega_{\mathrm{func}}=\lambda\log n$ from singular learning theory~\cite{lau2023llc,watanabe2009slt},
$\Omega_{\mathrm{total}}(c)=\Omega_{\mathrm{struct}}(c)+(\mu_s/\mu_c)\lambda_c\log n$, where $\lambda_c$ is
the local learning coefficient (RLCT) of the contract's converged candidate; a non-negativity clamp and a
non-convergence guard keep a noisy $\hat\lambda$ from entering $J$. This prices the effective, not the
nominal, dimensionality of the fitted function, and is second-order to $\Omega_{\mathrm{struct}}$ on
realistic converged data.

\paragraph{Width $\Kstar$.} \code{select\_architecture} sweeps width and reads the marginal value of neurons;
the analytical certificate sets the price scale. \code{width\_sparsity.py} realizes a two-layer fit
$f(x)=\sum_k a_k\tanh(w_k^\top x+b_k)$ by conditional-gradient atom insertion, adding the neuron of maximal
residual correlation while the dual certificate $\eta=A^\ast q/\lambda$, $\lVert\eta\rVert_\infty\le1$, is
unsaturated (\code{dual\_certificate.py}, \code{tv\_certificate.py}). A boundary-extension guard widens the
grid when the optimum sits on its edge.

\paragraph{Depth $\Lstar$.} \code{machinery/priced\_depth.py}: measure the depth curve $S(L)$ (validation
loss) over seeds and add layers while the per-layer marginal exceeds the price,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:depth-rule}
\Lstar=\max\Big\{L:\ -\big(S(L)-S(L{-}1)\big) > \mu\Big\},
\end{equation}
or, price-free, the last depth whose marginal clears $n_{\mathrm{se}}$ standard errors
(\code{significant\_elbow}, $n_{\mathrm{se}}{=}2$ the empirically safe bar). \code{select\_architecture\_by\_area}
minimizes total area (width$\times$depth) subject to a within-noise accuracy tolerance and extends depth past
the grid boundary up to a cap when the deeper column beats the noise floor.

\paragraph{Mixture sparsity.} \code{sparsity\_priced\_alpha.py}: charge the in-layer mixture its description
length so a layer keeps extra primitives only when they pay,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:alpha-price}
J(\alphapar)=R(\alphapar)+\mu\,\Omega(\alphapar),\qquad
\Omega(\alphapar)=H(w)\ \text{or}\ \lVert w\rVert_0^{\mathrm{soft}},\quad w=\softmax(\alphapar),
\end{equation}
driving $w$ toward a simplex vertex (a single primitive) unless the fit rewards a genuine mixture. This is
the deployment analogue of the width and depth charges: one more $\Omega$-term in \eqref{eq:action}.

\paragraph{Spectral modes.} \code{spectral\_selection.py}: for the operator contract, price the number of
retained Fourier modes $m$ by the same marginal-value rule, so spectral capacity is selected, not fixed.

\paragraph{Price $\mu$.} \code{price\_selection.py}: $\mu$ is the exchange rate in the scalarized
multi-objective $(\min R,\min\Omega)$; sweeping $\mu$ traces the Pareto frontier, and an ``elbow'' / L-curve
criterion picks the operating point. $\mu{=}0$ is pure fit.

\section{Deriving \texorpdfstring{$\alphapar$}{alpha}, co-adaptation, and discretization}
\label{sec:alpha}

\paragraph{$\alphapar$ as a Gibbs measure.} \code{gibbs\_alpha.py} identifies the softmax mixture weight as
the stationary measure of a free-energy flow on the simplex: with free energy
$\F_{\alphapar}=\langle\alphapar,\Psi\rangle-\tfrac1\beta H(\alphapar)$, the minimizer is the Gibbs form
$w_p\propto e^{-\beta\Psi_p}$, its Fisher--Rao gradient flow is the replicator equation, and DARTS~\cite{liu2019darts} is that
flow in a mildly preconditioned metric. The variational principle forces the per-primitive energy $\Psi_p$
to be the \emph{solo} marginal risk.

\paragraph{Co-adaptation.} \code{coadapt.py}: in a supernet all primitives run and mix, so their gradients
couple and a high-capacity primitive is over-credited. The fix reads the \emph{solo} marginal (each
primitive scored alone) as the selection signal and arbitrates a clean single-primitive winner, aligning the
selection energy with the $\alphapar$-derivation above.

\paragraph{Bilevel selection.} \code{bilevel.py}: weights are trained on a $w$-split and $\alphapar$ on a
disjoint $\alphapar$-split (three-way split with a held-out test), removing the training-loss bias that
selects capacity; \code{discretize\_and\_finetune} then collapses the mixture to its argmax primitive and
fine-tunes, converting a soft selection into a deployed discrete architecture. \code{robust\_discretize.py}
additionally collapses a redundant depth (adjacent layers selecting the same primitive) when doing so does
not raise the loss beyond noise.

\paragraph{Singular complexity.} \code{singular\_complexity.py}: the classical $\Omega=\tfrac{k}{2}\log n$
(parameter count) is replaced, where a local minimum is genuinely reached, by the local learning coefficient
(RLCT) $\lambda$ of singular learning theory~\cite{watanabe2009slt,lau2023llc}, $\Omega=\lambda\log n$; a negative measured $\lambda$ signals
non-convergence rather than low complexity, so the estimate is only used at a converged minimum and only
comparatively across independently-converged architectures.

\section{Read-outs and heads}
\label{sec:readout}

The top representation $h^{(L)}$ is mapped to a prediction by a contract-appropriate head $g$.
\emph{Sequence}: \code{last} $h^{(L)}_T$ (recall/copy), \code{mean} $\tfrac1T\sum_t h^{(L)}_t$
(classification), or \code{flatten} $\mathrm{vec}(h^{(L)})$ (position-aware), then a linear map to $n_{\mathrm{out}}$;
optional deep supervision attaches an early linear head to each layer. \emph{Graph/set}: a permutation-invariant
pool over nodes/elements $\bigoplus\in\{\text{mean},\text{sum},\text{max}\}$ to a graph/set vector, then linear.
\emph{Equivariant}: an \emph{invariant} read-out of the scalar channels (and vector norms) so the target is
$\SO$-invariant (energies) or, for vector targets, a type-1 output. \emph{Grid}: global pool over the spatial
axes then linear. \emph{Operator}: the field itself is the output (a $1{\times}1$ projection to the target
channels on the grid). A symbolic read-out option (\code{core/symbolic\_readout.py}) fits a closed-form
expression to the pooled features when an interpretable law is sought.

\paragraph{Auxiliary pipelines.} Beyond the eight forward contracts, \code{core/} provides the
symmetry-discovery pipeline (linear/$\SO$ and nonlinear-latent breaking tests, canonicalization,
metric/group discovery), the interpretability stack (feature attribution, the inspectable KAN radial curves
of \S\ref{sec:equiv}), and the information-theoretic diagnostics (IB~\cite{tishby2015ib}/RG-flow~\cite{mehta2014rg}, correlation length, mean-field
and exponent estimates) used to instrument the selections above. A family of singular/thermodynamic
read-outs sits on the same objects (\code{machinery/}): the local learning coefficient $\lambda$ at the
optimum and its training trajectory $\lambda(t)$; the single free-energy \emph{form}
$F=\langle\rho,E\rangle-\beta^{-1}H(\rho)$ with its three decoupled temperatures ($\beta_W=1/\log n$ at the
weights, $\beta_A$ at the primitive readout, $\beta_C=1$ at the contracts) and a consistency guard; the
response susceptibility (readout specific heat and the first-order contract transitions in the price); and
the effective-dimension ledger, one participation-ratio functional $1/\sum p^2$ read across the
coarse-graining axis (data modes, primitive mixture) with $\lambda$ as the model-level cap. All are pure
read-outs that change no selection; they are catalogued in the implementation report.


\section*{Provenance and references}
\code{ilmarinen} is an integrative repackaging: each primitive and selection rule realizes an idea from the
published literature, recombined under the common action $J=R+\mu\Omega$ and the meta-cell relaxation
\eqref{eq:cell}. The citations attached to the primitive tables and selection operators above point to the
originating works; the consolidated list follows. The package's own contribution is the unification and the
honest empirical auditing of when each component pays for itself, not the primitives themselves.
